India Union Budget 2026 27 Overview

Fresh off the press on 1 February 2026, India’s latest financial plan landed in the Lok Sabha through Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman - a milestone as her ninth straight turn at the podium. Though framed as routine, it quietly sketches where rupees will flow across roads, factories, clinics, and digital systems next. Not just numbers, really, but choices shaped around lifting towns, easing taxes, backing small hands, building tools, sparking industry, nudging progress. While details shift yearly, this one holds steady to long-term bets without fanfare. Behind spreadsheets lies a push - subtle, unpolished - toward stronger bones beneath the economy.

🧾 1. What Is the Union Budget?

Every year, India lays out its money plan through the Union Budget. This report shows how much cash officials expect to collect. Spending plans come into focus too. From April first until March thirty-first, these numbers guide decisions. Money coming in gets tallied alongside what will go out.

A mix of tax strategies, public funding choices, support payments, aid programs, also changes to how money moves through society shapes efforts to steer national growth.

💡 2. Big Priorities and Policy Directions

Rail Expansion and Big Spending Drive

Fresh money poured into public projects hits ₹12.2 lakh crore, a jump near 9%. Growth gets a nudge, employment tagged along.

Faster train routes across seven regions now set to link big urban centers.

Fresh routes on rivers plus new paths for cargo aim to speed up transport networks.

Faster growth often rests on sturdy roads, wires, lights. Building them first makes room for business to move. Not dreams, but steel and concrete open paths forward. Power grids, ports - these shape what comes next. Progress leans on such bones. Without them, speed stays out of reach.

💼 4. Taxation and Financial Market Reforms

Income Tax Made Simple

A fresh start begins April 1, 2026 - India's updated Income Tax Act takes over then, swapping out the outdated 1961 version. Simpler returns are now built into how it works. ET Now reported this shift earlier.

Income tax brackets stay unchanged. Fortune India notes the move.

Corporate and Investment Tax Rates

Firms now face a bottomed-out Minimum Alternate Tax at 14%, locked in as their closing liability under the updated rules. This shift seals MAT as the ultimate payment for qualifying companies. LatestLY notes the rate change takes effect without further additions. Finality marks the update - no extra layers tagged on afterward.

Until 2047, overseas firms running cloud services via local data hubs get tax breaks.
