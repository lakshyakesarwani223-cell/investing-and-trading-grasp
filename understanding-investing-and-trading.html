Understanding Investing and Trading

By: Purav

To understand trading and investing, first you need to clearly understand what investing is and what trading is.

1. Investing

The word investing means to invest (nivesh). It is a type of financial activity where patience plays the most important role—the more patience you have, the more potential returns you can earn.

Investing is a process in which we use our hard-earned money to buy assets such as stocks/shares, gold, silver, and cryptocurrencies, with the goal of earning profits in the future.

It is a long-term strategy, where wealth is built gradually, and over time investors can earn significant profits.

2. Trading

Trading is similar to investing in the sense that you buy and sell assets like stocks/shares, cryptocurrencies, gold, and silver to make a profit. However, trading is different from investing in terms of time period, risk, and strategy.

Trading is mainly of three types:

  • Swing Trading
  • Intraday Trading
  • Futures and Options (F&O) Trading

Swing Trading

In swing trading, you buy assets and hold them for a short period, usually from a few days to a few weeks, and sometimes up to three months.

Example:
If you buy one share of XYZ company at ₹100 and sell it at ₹107 after two weeks, you earn a profit of ₹7 per share.

Intraday Trading

In intraday trading, buying and selling happen on the same day. You cannot hold positions overnight.

Leverage is used in intraday trading, where brokers allow traders to trade with more money than they actually have, usually up to 5× leverage in India.

Example:
If you have ₹20,000 and use 5× leverage, you can trade shares worth ₹1,00,000.

Futures and Options (F&O) Trading

In F&O trading, you do not buy actual shares but trade contracts of shares or indices. These instruments require less capital but involve higher risk.

Where Can You Trade or Invest?

  • Stock Market: Buy shares of companies like Reliance through registered brokers.
  • Cryptocurrency Market: Trade cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH).
  • Forex Market: Forex trading is illegal for retail traders in India and is not covered here.

Advantages of Trading and Investing

  • Can be a side income or main income source
  • Helps in wealth creation over long-term experience
  • Helps beat inflation
  • Secures financial future and lifestyle
